Who we are

A reliable trade bridge between buyers worldwide and Chinese factories

We are a B2B trading agency based in Jinan, the capital of China's Shandong province. We operate as a single layer connecting international buyers with a network of vetted Chinese factories.

Our backbone is four sectors we know deeply: trucks, construction machinery, agricultural machinery and medical supplies. In each sector we build our own supplier network and verify factories ourselves rather than relying on intermediaries.

You deal with one accountable team that speaks Mandarin and English, and carries your file from the first enquiry through to the container reaching your destination port.

How it works

From your enquiry to the factory gate, in five clear steps

A structured, documented process. You always know who is handling your file, which stage it is at, and what happens next.

1

Send your enquiry

Send your list, product photos or equipment type by form, WhatsApp or email. We reply within one business day.

2

Matching & quotation

We match your requirement to vetted factories and collect three competing quotations with MOQ and lead time.

3

Sample & verification

Where needed we arrange samples or request factory photos and measurements, and confirm certificates before you commit.

4

Negotiation & contract

We negotiate price, payment terms and delivery, then issue a clear bilingual contract that protects both sides.

5

Production & quality

We follow production milestones, run pre-shipment inspection at the factory and attach a photo inspection report.

6

Shipping & delivery

We book the freight, prepare export documents, insurance and invoice, and track through to arrival at your port.

Why Sardia

Why buyers choose Sardia

The sourcing market is full of intermediaries and unverifiable promises. We built our process specifically to remove that ambiguity.

Audited factories, not names

Every factory in our network has been visited or verified by our own team. Audit notes and certificates sit in your project file before you pay.

Factory-direct pricing

We deal with the factory directly, without a chain of agents. You get the real wholesale price and avoid the margin an intermediary takes from your invoice.

Pre-shipment inspection

Pre-shipment checks or an independent third-party inspection, with a documented photo report attached to every order.

Complete shipping

After-sales support

Shipping is not the end. We follow up on spare parts, obtain translated manuals from the factory and help you with warranty claims.

Frequently asked

Questions buyers ask us first

You deal with us as a sourcing agency, which is written into the contract and agreed with the factory. We do not resell goods as our own stock; we act as the channel so you contract with the factory directly, and we take our commission from the factory in return for sourcing, documentation and quality control. That is why the price you receive is the real wholesale price, and why responsibility is clear in the contract.
It depends on the product. For most heavy machinery a sensible quantity is a single unit, while some consumable parts need larger runs. We state it early in the quotation, and we will always suggest a mixed alternative that combines different items to reach a practical order value.
Typically 25–35 days for manufacturing after order confirmation and the first payment, then 15–35 days for sea freight or 5–12 days by air, depending on destination. Timelines differ for large projects needing special cargo. We send a clear schedule with every quotation.
Three levels. First, factory verification and certificate checks before contracting. Second, a pre-shipment inspection against a specification checklist with a photo report. Third, an independent third-party inspection if you request it. Any material finding stops the shipment until it is resolved before shipping.
Normally 30% advance by bank transfer and 70% against a copy of the bill of lading, or a letter of credit for larger orders. Other arrangements are possible depending on order size and the buyer's country. We send banking details immediately after order confirmation.
We provide the complete document set: commercial invoice, bill of lading, certificate of origin, packing list and conformity certificates. We also help translate documents and check them against your country's import requirements. Customs clearance itself is under your authority and we cannot speak on your behalf, but our team is available to answer your broker's questions during clearance.
