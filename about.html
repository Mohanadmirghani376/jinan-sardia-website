Our story

From trading company to sourcing partner

We started in 2012 as a small trading company serving local construction projects, buying excavators and trucks for contractors across Shandong. Over time customers began asking about the same products for their own contacts overseas.

In 2016 Jinan Sardia International Supply Chain was formally established under a plain, direct name: we connect buyers worldwide with vetted Chinese factories. Today we work with more than 300 vetted factories and serve customers in 40 countries — from contractors in the Gulf to importers across North Africa, Asia and Europe.

What has not changed: we still work with a deliberately limited number of selected suppliers, and we still complete any file ourselves before we promise it.

Our values

Four rules we do not compromise on

Honest about cost

We tell you the real price and explain where the value goes. No hidden fees that appear at the end.

Clear on specification

What is in the sample is what arrives. We verify specifications and send photos before any commitment.

Committed to dates

We give a realistic deadline and never promise what we cannot deliver. Any delay is flagged early with an alternative.

Respect in business

We deal respectfully with you and with the factory. Trust is a trade measured in years, and it should not be drained in one argument.
